      What changed in the file

        Upload it right

        1. Upload in TikTok Studio from a browser. On your phone, open Chrome, tap ⋮ and turn on Desktop site. The TikTok app re-encodes videos before uploading.
        2. Post the patched file exactly as saved. Trimming, TikTok sounds, filters or text added in TikTok make it compress again.
        3. Set visibility to Everyone, not Only me. If you see an HD or high-quality upload option, turn it on.
        4. A length of 0:00 in your gallery is normal after Passthrough. The video still plays.

        Export settings that work best

        CodecH.264 (turn HEVC off)
        Resolution1080 × 1920
        Frame rate30 or 60 fps, constant
        Bitrate10–20 Mb/s
        AudioAAC, 48 kHz

        Keep it one tap away

        • Install: in Chrome tap ⋮, then Install app (or Add to Home screen). After that it works offline.
        • Share straight in: once installed, share a video from your gallery or CapCut and pick Keepframe.
        • iPhone: in Safari tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Sharing into the app isn't supported on iPhone.

        Passthrough reproduces the Editing News patcher as published open source by buwryme. Density follows NoBlur's frame-density inflation. FPS follows 120fps-method. Re-encoding uses your phone's own video encoder through WebCodecs. TikTok can change how it processes uploads at any time, so results vary between accounts and over time.
